Interlocked clusters

Zero-net items (fluids, intermediates nobody wants) tie recipes together. Most can be balanced top-down, one producer at a time. A byproduct with no producer of its own cannot: its balance becomes an equation among recipes you have already set. The clusters below are the recipes joined by such equations.
